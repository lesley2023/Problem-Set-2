The sample contains 1,129 monthly observations of annual real returns from December 1927 through December 2021, identical to Q4(d).
All 21 fixed full-sample betas are reused from Q4(d) and verified against its original first-pass code.
Each monthly cross-section uses one beta regressor and no intercept.
The pricing errors are averages of the monthly cross-sectional residuals.
The maximum absolute monthly OLS first-order-condition residual is $1.017\times10^{-14}$.
Standard errors follow the specified constant-only time-series HAC calculation, with fixed estimated betas.
The premium-series pilot is $m=7$, $C=2.4174$,
and $L=\lceil CT^{1/3}\rceil=\lceil 25.1714\rceil=26$.
Each pricing-error series selects its own lag, reported in the table.
The monthly premium and all 21 monthly pricing-error series are saved in
\texttt{results/q4e\_monthly\_risk\_premia.csv} and
\texttt{results/q4e\_monthly\_pricing\_errors.csv}, respectively.
