For the centered moments $u_t=g_t(\hat\gamma)-\bar g_T(\hat\gamma)$,
define $\hat c_\ell=T^{-1}\sum_{t=\ell+1}^T u_tu_{t-\ell}$.
The Newey--West (1994) Bartlett plug-in procedure uses
\[
  m=\left\lfloor4(T/100)^{2/9}\right\rfloor,\quad
  s_0=\hat c_0+2\sum_{\ell=1}^{m}\hat c_\ell,\quad
  s_1=2\sum_{\ell=1}^{m}\ell\hat c_\ell,
\]
\[
  C=1.1447\left[(s_1/s_0)^2\right]^{1/3},\qquad
  L=\left\lfloor CT^{1/3}\right\rfloor.
\]
Here $m=6$, $C=2.2693$, and $CT^{1/3}=23.6292$, giving $L=23$.
Thus the lag is data-dependent, with $m$ serving only as the pilot truncation.
With Bartlett weights $w_\ell=1-\ell/(L+1)$,
$\hat S=\hat c_0+2\sum_{\ell=1}^{L}w_\ell\hat c_\ell$.
The calculation uses no prewhitening or degrees-of-freedom adjustment,
matching the centered-moment HAC requested in the prompt. The plug-in
implementation follows \href{https://zeileis.codeberg.page/sandwich/reference/NeweyWest.html}{the documented Newey--West procedure}.
