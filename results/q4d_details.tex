The sample contains 1,129 monthly observations of annual real returns from December 1927 through December 2021, identical to Q4(b).
The requested constant-only regression uses gross $R_w$ and reports its sample mean as $\hat\lambda$.
The lambda-series pilot is $m=7$, $C=2.4496$,
and $L=\lceil CT^{1/3}\rceil=\lceil 25.5067\rceil=26$.
Each portfolio regression selects its own common lag from the sum of the two centered score components.
All HAC calculations retain Bartlett weights, $T-\ell$ divisors, and ceiling pilot/final rounding.

The regression intercepts and the normalized-SDF pricing errors have different definitions:
\[
\hat\alpha_j^{OLS}=\hat\mu_j-\hat\beta_j\bar R_w,\qquad
\hat e_j^{SDF}=\hat\mu_j-\hat\beta_j\hat\lambda_{SDF},\qquad
\hat\lambda_{SDF}=\hat b_{4b}\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(R_w).
\]
Here $\bar R_w=1.087734$, whereas $\hat\lambda_{SDF}=0.081817$.
The reported alphas are the requested gross-factor OLS intercepts, not the Q4(b) SDF errors.

At the 5\% level, 21 of the 21 intercepts have two-sided asymptotic-normal HAC $p$-values below 0.05.
The conventional GRS reference yields $p\approx 1.986\times10^{-807}$
and rejects the joint zero-intercept null at that level.
